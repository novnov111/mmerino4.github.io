Website Evaluations

Steam

  • Contrast: Steam uses strong contrast between its dark background, lighter text, and colorful game artwork. The game images stand out against the darker interface, making it easy for visitors to notice featured games and important buttons.
  • Repetition: Steam consistently repeats the same styles throughout the website. Game cards, buttons, menus, fonts, and colors follow a similar design, which makes the different sections feel connected and consistent.
  • Alignment: The content is organized into clear rows and columns. Game images, titles, prices, and other information are aligned within their sections, making the large amount of information easier to scan.
  • Proximity: Related information is kept close together. A game's image, title, price, and other details are grouped together, while navigation and filtering options are separated from the game listings. This makes it easier to understand which information belongs together.

Seiryu-Kan

  • Contrast: The website has weak visual contrast in several areas, with small text and a large amount of information competing for attention. The page does not have a strong visual hierarchy, making it difficult to tell which information is most important.
  • Repetition: The website does not maintain a strong consistent design throughout the page. Different sections use different styles, colors, and formatting, which makes the site feel less unified and makes some elements harder to recognize as belonging to the same website.
  • Alignment: Many elements appear crowded together instead of following a clean, consistent grid. Text, images, and other content do not always share clear edges, which makes the page feel disorganized and makes information harder to follow.
  • Proximity: Related information is not always grouped clearly, while unrelated information can appear close together. This makes it harder to determine which headings, images, and pieces of text belong together and forces the visitor to spend more time figuring out the page structure.
