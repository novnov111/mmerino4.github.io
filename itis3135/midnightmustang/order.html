How to Order

Ordering at Midnight Mustang takes about a minute, so you will spend more time playing than waiting. Here is how it works:

  1. Pick a cocoa from the menu and tell the cart which milk you want.
  2. Grab a board from the stack. Boards are free with any drink, and pieces are returned when you finish.
  3. Find a table and play. Your cup is ready in about two minutes, and refills are half price.

Cash and cards are both accepted. Tell the cart ahead of time if you have an allergy, because the menu includes milk, nuts, and cayenne.
