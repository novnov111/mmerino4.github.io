About

Midnight Mustang is run by Michael Merino, a former Marine who now studies computer science at UNCC. Between classes he works on his car, plays chess, and tries to finish his homework. This business puts two of those hobbies in one place.

The cart is small on purpose. One person, six cocoas, and a stack of chessboards is enough to make a late night feel like a gathering instead of a chore.

The name comes from the mascot: Midnight for the hours the cart keeps, and Mustang for the horse that gallops across the chessboard as the knight.

“One more move before midnight.”

Midnight Mustang Cocoa & Chess is an imaginary business created by Michael Merino for ITIS3135.
