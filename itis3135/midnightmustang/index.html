Welcome to Midnight Mustang Cocoa & Chess, the little cart that serves hot chocolate and a chessboard to anyone still awake after dark. This is an imaginary business, but everything about it is built to feel real: a short menu, honest hours, and a place to sit and think your next move.

Every cup is made by hand from a menu of just six cocoas, so there is never a long line and never a hard choice. Pick a cup, pull up a stool, and challenge a stranger or a friend to a game. The boards are free with any drink.

New here? Start with the menu, check the hours, or read how to order.
