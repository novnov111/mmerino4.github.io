Location

The cart parks near the UNCC campus on weeknights and moves to a busy corner uptown on weekends. Look for the amber awning and the chessboards set out on small tables.

The cart is easy to find: it is the only place on the block with steam rising into the night air. If you can smell chocolate, you are close.

Because this is an imaginary business, the exact spots are made up. A real version of Midnight Mustang would post each night's location so players always know where to find a game.
